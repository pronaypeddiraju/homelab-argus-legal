homelab-argus

A private, single-user automation utility.

homelab-argus is a personal-use application. It runs on infrastructure operated by its owner and is used by exactly one person — the owner — to read their own Google data.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's own Gmail messages, read-only.
  • Reads the owner's own Google Calendar, read-only.
  • Reads the owner's own Google Drive files, read-only.

Its purpose is personal record-keeping: summarising receipts, tracking purchases, and reviewing the owner's own schedule.

What it does not do

  • It cannot send, modify, or delete email, calendar entries, or Drive files.
  • It has no other users, no sign-up, no accounts, and is not distributed.
  • It is not offered as a product or service to anyone else.

Data handling is described in the privacy policy.

Personal-use application. Not distributed, not for sale, no third-party access.
